## 2. Common content (every message)

**Who we are:** Subterra, an open research platform for evidence-backed interpretation of subsurface sensing data. It was built with conservative validation: pre-registered experiments, independent ground truth, and failures reported, not hidden.

**What we have done:**
- Ingested and benchmarked open GPR datasets (BAM concrete specimens, TU1208/IFSTTAR, 4TU utility surveys).
- Published negative results honestly. Our detector work stopped because **no public soil dataset has raw traces, registered coordinates and independent object truth together**.

**What we ask, in order of preference:**
- (a) existing **raw** GPR data (native format, not images), with
- (b) the associated **independent ground truth** (as-built or surveyed positions and depths, plus any verified-empty areas), or
- (c) access to a **controlled survey**: a site, or a borrowed instrument, possibly as a student project.

**What we offer:**
- co-authorship or acknowledgement as appropriate;
- an open, reproducible evaluation report on their data;
- conversion and processing code;
- any licence terms they need (non-commercial, NDA, embargo);
- no redistribution without permission.

**Cost:** we have no budget and ask for no-cost access. We would not expect them to bear costs either; any exchange would be in kind.
